Insurance & payment

Common questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your plan details and we'll confirm coverage before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our membership plan covers two cleanings, exams and X-rays a year, plus 15% off other treatment. We also offer interest-free payment plans.

Can you help if I'm anxious?

Absolutely. Tell us when you book. We'll go at your pace and can offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation where appropriate.

How long does a first visit take?

Around 60 minutes, including an exam, X-rays, a cleaning if needed, and time to talk through your plan.

Not sure what you need?

Book a free consult and we'll help you work out the best next step, with no obligation.
