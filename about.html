About us

Dentistry with a human touch

We started Lumen with a simple idea: a trip to the dentist should feel as calm as a visit to a friend's home, with world-class care behind it.

Our story

Built for people who dread the dentist

Dr. Amira Rahman opened Lumen in 2011 after years of watching patients put off care because they were anxious, confused by pricing, or felt rushed. So she designed the opposite: longer appointments, plain-language explanations, and a written estimate before any treatment.

Today we're a team of 14 dentists, hygienists and coordinators who still hold to that promise for every patient, from toddlers to grandparents.

  1. 2011

    Doors open

    A two-chair studio with one dentist and a big idea.

  2. 2016

    Family care grows

    A dedicated pediatric suite and our first orthodontist join.

  3. 2021

    Going digital

    3D scanning, same-day crowns and online booking.

  4. Today

    8,000+ patients

    Still unhurried, still personal, still local.
