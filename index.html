Frequently asked questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your plan details and we'll confirm your coverage before your visit.

What if I don't have insurance?

Our in-house membership plan covers two cleanings, exams and X-rays a year, plus 15% off other treatment. We also offer interest-free payment plans.

I'm anxious about dental visits. Can you help?

Absolutely. Let us know when you book. We'll go at your pace, explain each step, and can offer nitrous oxide or oral sedation where appropriate.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications. You can fill in forms online beforehand to save time.

At what age should my child first see a dentist?

We recommend a first visit by age one, or within six months of the first tooth appearing.
