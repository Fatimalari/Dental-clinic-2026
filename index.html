Our services

  1. Every smile starts with a conversation. We listen, examine and show you what we see on screen, then give you a clear plan and cost estimate before any treatment begins.

    • Full oral examination
    • Digital X-rays & 3D scans
    • Personalised treatment plan
    • Transparent cost estimate

  2. Routine care that keeps small problems small. Gentle check-ups and cleanings, plus quick treatment when something needs attention.

    • Check-ups & cleanings
    • Tooth-coloured fillings
    • Gum care
    • Sealants & fluoride
    • Emergency appointments

  3. Rebuild strength, comfort and function after damage or decay, with restorations designed to look and feel natural.

    • Crowns & bridges
    • Inlays & onlays
    • Root canal treatment
    • Dentures

  4. Precise, gentle procedures planned with 3D imaging, with sedation options and clear aftercare so you heal comfortably.

    • Tooth extractions
    • Wisdom teeth removal
    • Dental implants
    • Bone grafting

  5. Straighter teeth at any age, with discreet options that fit your lifestyle and check-ins that fit your schedule.

    • Clear aligners
    • Fixed braces
    • Retainers
    • Early orthodontic checks

  6. Refine the colour, shape and harmony of your smile, planned together with a digital preview before we begin.

    • Teeth whitening
    • Porcelain veneers
    • Composite bonding
    • Smile makeover
